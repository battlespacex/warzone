WHY WE BUILT IT

ONE PICTURE,
EVIDENCE ATTACHED

Open-source military information is abundant and scattered. StratOps exists to bring it into one geographic picture and keep its evidence attached.

THE PROBLEM

SCATTERED
BY DEFAULT

Each public source answers a narrow question. Where is this aircraft? What did the navy publish? Is that airspace restricted? Investigators then join the answers by hand, across tabs, time zones and file formats.

The details that matter most are the easiest to lose along the way: who said it, when, and how sure they were.

StratOps is our answer. Bring the sources onto one 3D globe, and keep those details attached to every item.

HOW WE BUILD

THREE RULES
WE BUILD BY

PRINCIPLEGeography first

Location is the common key. Putting unlike data on one globe lets you compare it in space and time, without opening six tools.

PRINCIPLEEvidence attached

Source, age and confidence travel with every item. Observations, reports and predictions never share one symbol.

PRINCIPLEHonest limits

We would rather show a gap than hide one. Where coverage, freshness or access varies, the product and this site say so.
