EVERY MARKER
SHOWS ITS WORK

Observed tracks, published reports and orbital predictions mean different things. Review the evidence behind the symbol.

Read the intelligence
Observed

A timestamp, not total coverage

Aircraft and vessel positions can be stale, partial or missing. Check source time and identity evidence first.

Reported

A report, not a live position

A deployment or strike report says what a source claimed and when. It does not say where an asset is now.

Predicted

A model, not a sighting

Orbits and range overlays are calculations. They do not prove sensor activity or coverage.
