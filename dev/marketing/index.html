09 / Intelligence transparency

Context includes
what we don't know.

Coverage is source-dependent

No public feed provides complete visibility of all military assets. Observations may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Absence from the map does not establish absence of activity.

Observed is different from predicted

Reported operating regions, observed coordinates and predicted orbital positions have different meanings. StratOps preserves these distinctions; it does not manufacture live positions.

Classification has limits

Identity matches and military confidence depend on available identifiers and evidence. Unverified reports and unknown statuses are not confirmations of safety or activity.

An analysis tool, not a navigation service

StratOps is an open-source intelligence environment. It is not intended for navigation, flight planning or safety-critical operational decisions.
