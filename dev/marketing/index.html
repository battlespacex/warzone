06/ SOURCE TRANSPARENCY

INTELLIGENCE REQUIRES DISTINCTION

NOT ALL POINTS
MEAN THE SAME.

StratOps is built for situational awareness, not certainty where the underlying source cannot provide it. Observation, reporting and prediction each have a different evidentiary meaning.

01 / OBSERVED

Data that was seen.

Telemetry-visible positions and other published observations can be incomplete, delayed or intentionally absent.

02 / REPORTED

Information that was stated.

OSINT reporting and attributed events require source review and may be revised as new evidence emerges.

03 / PREDICTED

Positions that were modeled.

Orbital positions, modeled ranges and inferred patterns are not confirmations of direct observation.

Questions about coverage?

Public-source visibility is not continuous or universal. Explore the data principles below.

Is every military aircraft or vessel visible?

No. Coverage depends on public telemetry, available identifiers, provider access and regional conditions. An absent track does not prove an asset is absent.

Is the orbital display a live satellite camera?

No. Orbital tracks use public orbital elements and predicted positions. Satellite observation imagery and its metadata are a separate feature.

Do NOTAM and GNSS layers provide safety guidance?

No. StratOps shows available published notices and source-dependent interference observations for analysis. It is not an official aviation, maritime, navigation or emergency service.

Does the platform provide official military intelligence?

No. StratOps is an independent OSINT-informed analysis and visualization platform, not affiliated with a government, military, intelligence service or emergency authority.
