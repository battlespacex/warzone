Focus active conflicts, strategic regions, flashpoints, escalation indicators,

Regional Intelligence Filtering

Acceptance of These Rules
By accessing or using StratOps, you acknowledge and agree to these Rules of
Engagement and Terms of Use.

If you do not agree, discontinue use of the platform.

Information Sources
StratOps may collect, aggregate, summarize, classify, visualize, or reference
information from publicly available and partner-accessible sources, including:

- Open-source intelligence
- Public aviation and maritime data
- Public orbital element data
- Government publications
- News and media reporting
- Public telemetry and event feeds
- Public satellite and geospatial information
- Event-linked satellite observation metadata
- User-submitted or community-sourced observations where applicable

Displayed information may be incomplete, delayed, inaccurate, inferred,
duplicated, disputed, unavailable, or later corrected.

Users are responsible for independently verifying information before relying on
it.

No Operational Authority
StratOps does not issue:

- Military orders
- Security instructions
- Emergency directives
- Airspace advisories
- Maritime navigation instructions
- Evacuation notices
- Civil-defense guidance
- Official threat warnings
- Sensor-tasking instructions
- Targeting guidance

Nothing displayed on StratOps should be interpreted as official direction from
any government, military, intelligence organization, emergency agency, aviation
authority, maritime authority, public institution, or safety authority.

Accuracy and Reliability
StratOps makes reasonable efforts to improve data quality, filtering,
classification, and operational readability, but does not guarantee:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Reliability
- Timeliness
- Availability
- Source continuity
- Correct attribution
- Correct asset identity
- Correct classification
- Operational relevance

Tracks, events, alerts, reports, locations, movements, classifications,
satellite context, orbital estimates, and other visualizations may contain
errors or omissions.

Users assume responsibility for how platform information is interpreted or used.

Open-Source Intelligence Disclaimer
StratOps uses publicly accessible, open-source, or partner-accessible
information unless clearly stated otherwise.

StratOps does not claim access to classified, restricted, protected,
confidential, or government-controlled intelligence.

Platform-generated labels, summaries, classifications, reports, and
visualizations are informational interpretations of available source data and
are not authoritative intelligence products.

Aircraft and Naval Tracking Disclaimer
Aircraft and naval records depend on available public or partner-accessible
telemetry and may be delayed, incomplete, misidentified, unavailable, or
affected by coverage limitations.

The absence of a displayed aircraft or vessel does not confirm that no activity
is present in the area.

Satellite and Orbital Disclaimer
Orbital Assets are predicted positions propagated from public orbital elements.
They are not live satellite detections, classified orbital intelligence,
real-time sensor tracks, or confirmation of active surveillance coverage.

Satellite Observations are contextual imagery records tied to events or
reporting periods. They do not independently confirm damage, attribution, cause,
live activity, or operational outcome.

Hotspot and Event Disclaimer
A marker, hotspot, activity area, or cluster represents available source data
and platform classification. It is not automatic confirmation that every
underlying report is accurate.

A hotspot may represent one event or multiple nearby events.

Reports Disclaimer
Operational reports summarize available cached data, aggregated activity, and
source-derived context. Reports may be incomplete or unavailable when feeds,
processing services, storage, or public report access are unavailable.

Reports are briefing aids and must not be treated as official intelligence
assessments, legal findings, safety instructions, or command documents.

Alerts and Audio Disclaimer
Alert banners and siren sounds are interface indicators for relevant
public-warning-style signals. They are not official emergency broadcasts and
must not be used as the sole basis for evacuation, security, travel, or safety
decisions.

- Research

- Product demonstrations
- Authorized evaluation

All use must comply with applicable laws, regulations, source restrictions, and
platform terms.
