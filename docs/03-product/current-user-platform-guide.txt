BATTLESPACEX STRATOPS - CURRENT USER PLATFORM GUIDE
Last updated: 2026-07-28

Purpose:
This document explains the current StratOps platform in user-facing language for operators, analysts, demos, and non-developer users.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

StratOps is a Cesium-based operational map for OSINT-informed conflict, military activity, aircraft, naval, space, satellite, and infrastructure visualization.

The interface combines:
- 3D globe navigation with optional 2D scene support
- region selection and current-region filtering
- live and near-live event layers
- aircraft and naval trackers
- military base and strategic asset overlays
- orbital asset estimates from public CelesTrak GP elements
- event-linked satellite observation and imagery context
- GNSS, airspace, cyber, alert, thermal, radar, range, and hotspot layers
- operational popups, widgets, report viewing, and premium layer gating

Important limitation:
StratOps is not an official warning network, classified intelligence product, or independent verification authority. Map items are visualizations of available source data and may be incomplete, delayed, inferred, duplicated, or wrong.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. MAIN MAP LAYERS
------------------------------------------------------------

Current layer panel:

- Shelling / Ground Strikes: artillery, shelling, and uncategorized impact reports.
- Missiles & Rockets: missile, rocket, ballistic, cruise, launch, and impact activity.
- Drone / UAV Activity: drone sightings, loitering munitions, UAV, and drone-delivered activity.
- Air-Delivered Strikes: aircraft-delivered strike and bombing reports.
- Aircraft Tracker: live or near-live military aircraft telemetry and movement. Premium.
- Airspace Status: regional closure or restriction status widget. Premium.
- Naval Activity: military naval contacts and vessel-linked signals. Premium.
- Military Bases: known military base and installation locations. Premium.
- GNSS Jamming: sanitized GNSS/GPS interference cells and navigation anomaly context. Premium.
- Radar / Threat Ranges: estimated fighter, AWACS, naval-defense, and SAM/radar envelopes. Premium.
- Radar Sweepers: animated sweep sectors for active radar and air-defense visualization. Premium.
- Alerts & Sirens: warning banners, sirens, active alerts, and urgent public warning signals.
- Cyber Operations: cyber threat and network disruption signals. Premium.
- Thermal / Fires: thermal anomalies, fires, heat signatures, and fire-related detections.
- Recon / Intelligence: reconnaissance, surveillance, and military observation reports.
- Seismic / Explosions: seismic, blast, and explosion-linked detections.
- Activity Areas: passive density circles and cluster context behind event markers.
- Orbital Assets: public orbital estimates for military-associated and dual-use satellites. Premium.
- Satellite Observations: available Sentinel/Copernicus-style image observations tied to events. Premium.
- Satellite Basemap: satellite basemap imagery on the globe.
- Raised Region: selected-region emphasis plate.
- Country Borders: country boundary overlay.

Orbital Assets and Satellite Observations are separate:
- Orbital Assets shows predicted satellite positions calculated from public orbital elements.
- Satellite Observations shows imagery/context records tied to events.
- A satellite position is not the same thing as a satellite image observation.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. ORBITAL ASSETS
------------------------------------------------------------

The Orbital Assets layer uses public CelesTrak orbital element data and satellite.js propagation in the browser. The backend endpoint is:

GET /api/satellites/military

The source query is:
https://celestrak.org/NORAD/elements/gp.php?GROUP=military&FORMAT=JSON

The system also reads required public SATCAT metadata from:
https://celestrak.org/pub/satcat.csv

Language used in the interface:
- Public orbital estimate
- Predicted position
- Publicly tracked
- Military-associated
- Dual-use
- Mission unconfirmed

Language not used:
- confirmed live satellite detection
- sensor coverage
- radar coverage
- surveillance coverage
- strike range

Normal view:
- lightweight satellite symbols
- restrained opacity and distance scaling
- no global labels by default
- no orbit paths, ground tracks, nadir lines, or footprints for every satellite

Hover view:
- compact public orbital information card
- satellite name, classification, confidence, country/owner, mission, NORAD ID, orbit class, altitude, speed, inclination, epoch, data age, and source

Click/focus view:
- selected satellite model when supported
- recent and predicted orbit arc
- sub-satellite ground track
- nadir line
- theoretical line-of-sight footprint
- focused satellite information card

The focused card clearly states that the position is propagated from public orbital elements and is not a direct sensor detection.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOTSPOT CLASSIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Hotspots are grouped labels for active events. A hotspot may represent one event or a cluster of nearby events.

Current hotspot categories:

- strike: direct strike, shelling, explosion, impact, artillery, or battlefield damage report.
- air_activity: fighters, AWACS, ISR/recon aircraft, tankers, bombers, helicopters, air-defense aircraft, or SAM/air-defense context.
- naval_activity: carrier, destroyer, frigate, corvette, cruiser, submarine, fleet, vessel, naval movement, patrol, or naval-linked activity.
- ground_activity: military activity that is not clearly air or naval, including troop movement, land forces, convoys, buildup, or unclear military reports.
- recon_intel: intelligence, reconnaissance, surveillance, observation, SIGINT/ELINT-style reporting, or situational reports.
- alert: air raid warnings, emergency alerts, sirens, restrictions, evacuation warnings, or urgent status changes.
- airspace: airspace closure, airspace restriction, NOTAM-style status, or air route disruption.
- cyber: cyberattack, hacking, network outage, digital infrastructure disruption, or cyber operation.
- thermal: thermal anomaly, fire, heat detection, or wildfire/fire-linked event.
- signal: signal/seismic/blast detection.
- seismic: seismic or explosion-linked detection.
- unknown_activity: generic activity when the event cannot be confidently classified.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. MILITARY BASE CLASSIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Current canonical military base types:

- airbase: air base, air force base, airfield, military airport, air station.
- naval: naval base, navy base, naval station, naval port, fleet base, submarine base.
- army: army base, fort, garrison, camp, barracks, armored/infantry/land-force installation.
- missile: missile site, ICBM site, ballistic missile site, SAM site, air-defense missile site, rocket-force site.
- cyber: cyber command, signals intelligence, communications command, electronic warfare command.
- joint: joint base, multi-service base, coalition base, NATO base, combined forces installation.
- hq: headquarters, command center, military command, defense/ministry command facility.
- space_launch: space launch site, cosmodrome, satellite launch facility, rocket launch center.
- radar: radar site, early-warning radar, tracking station, ISR radar, communications/radar station.
- drone: drone base, UAV/UAS facility, remotely piloted aircraft facility.
- weapons_storage: weapons storage, ammunition depot, nuclear storage, ordnance depot.
- training_logistics: training base, academy, training center, logistics base, supply depot.
- wmd: nuclear explosion/test site.
- unknown: military installation when the type is unclear.

Space launch sites and missile/ICBM sites are intentionally separate categories.

6. AIRCRAFT TRACKER
------------------------------------------------------------

The Aircraft Tracker always shows assets for the selected region. The widget no longer needs a region dropdown for ordinary use.

Current filters:
- country
- aircraft type

Widget card fields:
- title/callsign in uppercase
- type
- force/country/operator when available
- altitude
- speed
- heading
- last seen / status
- Focus or Unlock action

Aircraft tracker classifications include:
- fighter
- awacs
- recon
- isr
- tanker
- refueler
- transport
- logistics
- bomber
- helicopter
- drone/uav
- vip
- aircraft

Fighter records are identified through explicit type metadata and expanded model/name patterns when available. If the source does not provide enough aircraft detail, the system keeps a broader aircraft fallback instead of inventing a fighter label.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. SATELLITE OBSERVATIONS AND REPORTS
------------------------------------------------------------

Satellite Observations are contextual imagery records tied to events or report periods. They are separate from orbital tracking.

Current satellite observation behavior:
- Uses sanitized public metadata and image URLs only.
- May include provider, collection, acquisition time, resolution, cloud cover, and preview image when available.
- Does not claim live imagery, live detection, damage confirmation, attribution, or battle outcome.
- Reports may include satellite observation counts and a contextual satellite page when a cached observation is available.

If a report says satellite observations were available, that refers to rows in the event satellite observation table, not live satellite detection.

------------------------------------------------------------
9. AUTHENTICATION AND PREMIUM ACCESS
------------------------------------------------------------

Premium layer gates require the user to sign in before enabling selected high-value overlays.

Current premium-gated layers include:
- Aircraft Tracker
- Airspace Status
- Naval Activity
- Military Bases
- GNSS Jamming
- Radar / Threat Ranges
- Radar Sweepers
- Cyber Operations
- Orbital Assets
- Satellite Observations

If a premium layer is locked, clicking it opens the StratOps sign-in flow. Production authentication remains separate from staging authentication behavior.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. USER NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------

- A marker or hotspot is a visualization of available data, not automatic confirmation of every source claim.
- Some records have richer details than others.
- Aircraft and naval assets may use PNG icons or selected 3D models depending on active render settings.
- Orbital satellite positions are propagated estimates based on public orbital elements.
- Satellite observation imagery is contextual review material, not live sensor feed data.
- Users should verify critical findings through authoritative channels before making operational, safety, legal, or policy decisions.
