BATTLESPACEX STRATOPS - CURRENT USER PLATFORM GUIDE
Last updated: 2026-05-07

Purpose:
This document explains the current StratOps platform in user-facing language. It is for operators, analysts, demos, and non-developer users who need to understand what the map shows and how the main classifications work.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------

StratOps is a Cesium-based 3D operational map for conflict and military OSINT visualization.

The interface combines:
- 3D globe navigation
- region selection
- live and near-live event layers
- hotspot labels
- military base icons
- aircraft tracker
- naval tracker
- airspace, cyber, range, and radar-style widgets
- popups and status panels
- premium-gated layers for sensitive/high-value overlays

------------------------------------------------------------
2. MAIN MAP LAYERS
------------------------------------------------------------

The current layer panel includes:

- Strikes & Artillery: mapped strike, shelling, and impact reports.
- Missiles & Rockets: missile, rocket, ballistic missile, cruise missile, and related launch/impact activity.
- Drones / UAVs: drone, UAV, loitering munition, Shahed-style, and kamikaze drone activity.
- Air Strikes: air-delivered strike and bombing activity.
- Aircraft Tracker: live or near-live military aircraft telemetry and movement.
- Airspace Status: regional airspace closure, restriction, or status widget.
- Naval Activity: military naval contacts and vessel-linked signals.
- Military Bases: known military bases, military installations, and nuclear explosion/test sites.
- Radar / Threat Ranges: estimated fighter, AWACS, naval-defense, and SAM/radar coverage overlays.
- Radar Sweepers: animated sweep sectors for active radar and air-defense visualization.
- Alerts & Sirens: warning banners, sirens, active alert events, and urgent public warning signals.
- Cyber Operations: cyber threat, network disruption, cyberattack, and digital infrastructure events.
- Thermal / Fires: thermal anomalies, fires, heat signatures, and fire-related detections.
- Recon / Intelligence: reconnaissance, intelligence, surveillance, and general military observation reports.
- Seismic / Explosions: seismic, blast, and explosion-linked detections.
- Hotspot Labels: cluster labels for active event concentrations.
- Satellite Imagery: satellite basemap imagery on the globe.
- Country Borders: country boundary overlay.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOTSPOT CLASSIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Hotspots are grouped labels for active events. A hotspot may represent one event or a cluster of nearby events.

Current hotspot categories:

- strike: direct strike, shelling, explosion, impact, artillery, or battlefield damage report.
- air_activity: fighters, AWACS, ISR/recon aircraft, tankers, bombers, helicopters, air-defense aircraft, or SAM/air-defense context.
- naval_activity: carrier, destroyer, frigate, corvette, cruiser, submarine, fleet, vessel, naval movement, patrol, or naval-linked activity.
- ground_activity: military activity that is not clearly air or naval, including troop movement, land forces, convoys, buildup, or unclear military reports.
- recon_intel: intelligence, reconnaissance, surveillance, observation, SIGINT/ELINT-style reporting, or situational reports that do not fit a specific air/naval/ground bucket.
- alert: air raid warnings, emergency alerts, sirens, restrictions, evacuation warnings, or urgent status changes.
- airspace: airspace closure, airspace restriction, NOTAM-style status, or air route disruption.
- cyber: cyberattack, hacking, network outage, digital infrastructure disruption, or cyber operation.
- thermal: thermal anomaly, fire, heat detection, or wildfire/fire-linked event.
- signal: signal/seismic/blast detection. In the current layer router, signal routes to Seismic / Explosions.
- seismic: seismic or explosion-linked detection.
- unknown_activity: generic activity when the input category is default/activity or the event cannot be confidently classified.

How military/recon events are split:
- Aircraft, fighters, AWACS, ISR, tanker, bomber, helicopter, air defense, or SAM context becomes air_activity.
- Carrier, destroyer, frigate, corvette, cruiser, submarine, warship, naval, fleet, or vessel context becomes naval_activity.
- Military reports not clearly air/naval become ground_activity.
- Recon reports not clearly air/naval become recon_intel.

4. MILITARY BASE CLASSIFICATIONS
------------------------------------------------------------

Current canonical military base types:

- airbase: air base, air force base, airfield, military airport, air station.
- naval: naval base, navy base, naval station, naval port, fleet base, submarine base, marine/coast guard military base.
- army: army base, fort, garrison, camp, barracks, armored/infantry/land-force installation.
- missile: missile site, ICBM site, ballistic missile site, SAM site, air-defense missile site, rocket-force site, missile test range.
- cyber: cyber command, signals intelligence, communications command, electronic warfare command, cyber/space operations command where not a physical launch site.
- joint: joint base, multi-service base, coalition base, NATO base, combined forces installation.
- hq: headquarters, command center, military command, defense/ministry command facility.
- space_launch: space launch site, cosmodrome, satellite launch facility, rocket launch center. This is separate from missile/ICBM launch sites.
- radar: radar site, early-warning radar, tracking station, ISR radar, communications/radar station.
- drone: drone base, UAV/UAS facility, remotely piloted aircraft facility.
- weapons_storage: weapons storage, ammunition depot, nuclear storage, ordnance depot, arms depot, munitions depot.
- training_logistics: training base, academy, training center, logistics base, supply depot, maintenance depot, repair depot.
- wmd: nuclear explosion/test site.
- unknown: military installation when the type is unclear.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. MILITARY BASE POPUP DETAILS
------------------------------------------------------------

When a user clicks a military base marker, the popup can show:
- type
- country
- operator
- classification/size
- coordinates
- extra metadata fields when available

Extra fields are record-specific. Some bases have only basic information. Nuclear explosion/test sites and imported records may have additional display fields such as category, test/site details, notes, or external reference fields. The popup intentionally hides "Source" and "Source section" rows in the UI.

The popup follows the selected base as the map moves and hides when the selected base moves out of the browser viewport.

6. AIRCRAFT TRACKER TYPES
------------------------------------------------------------

Aircraft tracker classifications currently include:
- fighter
- awacs
- recon
- isr
- tanker
- refueler
- transport
- logistics
- logistic
- bomber
- helicopter
- drone/uav
- vip
- trainer
- aircraft

Common meaning:
- Fighter: combat jet or interceptor.
- AWACS: airborne warning and control aircraft.
- Recon/ISR: surveillance, reconnaissance, maritime patrol, intelligence aircraft.
- Tanker/Refueler: aerial refueling aircraft.
- Transport/Logistics: airlift, cargo, and support aircraft.
- Bomber: strategic or tactical bomber.
- Helicopter: rotary-wing aircraft.
- Drone/UAV: unmanned aircraft.
- VIP: government or command transport.
- Trainer: training aircraft.
- Aircraft: fallback when the platform cannot classify more specifically.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. NAVAL TRACKER TYPES
------------------------------------------------------------

Naval tracker classifications currently include:
- carrier
- amphibious
- cruiser
- destroyer
- frigate
- corvette
- intelligence
- ssbn
- ssn
- ssk
- aip_submarine
- submarine
- missile_boat
- patrol
- logistics
- minesweeper
- naval

Common meaning:
- Carrier: aircraft carrier or carrier group.
- Amphibious: amphibious assault, LHD/LHA/LPD/LSD/LST type vessel.
- Cruiser/Destroyer/Frigate/Corvette: surface combatants by class/role.
- Intelligence: SIGINT, ELINT, tracking, reconnaissance, surveillance, special mission vessel.
- SSBN: ballistic missile submarine.
- SSN: nuclear attack submarine.
- SSK: diesel-electric submarine.
- AIP Sub: air-independent-propulsion submarine.
- Submarine: fallback submarine class.
- Missile Boat: fast attack/missile craft.
- Patrol: patrol ship or coast guard cutter in military context.
- Logistics: oiler, replenishment, sealift, auxiliary, support ship.
- Minesweeper: mine countermeasure or minehunter.
- Naval: fallback when the platform knows it is naval but not the exact class.

------------------------------------------------------------
8. USER NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------

- A marker or hotspot is a visualization of available data, not automatic confirmation of every source claim.
- Some records have richer details than others.
- Military base records can have different metadata fields depending on source and type.
- Space launch sites and missile/ICBM sites are intentionally separate categories.
- Aircraft and naval assets may use PNG icons or GLB 3D models depending on active render settings.
