1. LINKEDIN LAUNCH / POSITIONING POST
------------------------------------------------------------

BattleSpaceX StratOps is being built as a briefing-grade 3D OSINT visualization platform for conflict, military activity, and strategic security signals.

Most tools show one slice of the picture: aircraft, vessels, incidents, bases, or analysis.

StratOps brings these layers into one operational globe:

- live/near-live aircraft tracking
- live naval activity
- GLB 3D aircraft and naval asset visualization
- curated military bases and installations
- strike, drone, missile, airspace, cyber, and alert layers
- radar/threat range overlays
- region-aware theater intelligence
- cinematic command-center UI for demos and briefings

The goal is not to replace specialist flight, vessel, or news platforms.
The goal is to turn fragmented open-source signals into a unified 3D operational awareness layer.

Built for OSINT visualization, defense-adjacent storytelling, media explainers, strategic demos, and high-impact briefings.

BattleSpaceX StratOps: from scattered signals to an operational picture.
