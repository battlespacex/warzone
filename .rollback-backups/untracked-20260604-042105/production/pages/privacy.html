Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 29, 2026

Overview

StratOps is a real-time, multi-domain intelligence visualization platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when users access StratOps, create or use an account, purchase a subscription, or interact with platform features.

Information We Collect

We may collect account, billing, usage, and technical information, including:

  • Name, email address, and account identifiers provided through BattlespaceX login.
  • Subscription status, plan, Stripe customer identifiers, and payment event metadata.
  • Device, browser, IP address, approximate location, logs, and security telemetry.
  • Feature interactions such as selected regions, widgets, and platform preferences.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. StratOps does not store full credit card numbers. Stripe may collect and process payment information according to its own privacy and security practices.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, maintain, and secure StratOps access.
  • Manage subscriptions, plan status, billing, receipts, and support.
  • Improve platform reliability, performance, and user experience.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, or operational issues.
  • Comply with legal, tax, payment, and security obligations.

Information Sharing

We may share limited information with service providers that help operate StratOps, including payment processors, hosting providers, analytics or monitoring tools, and security services. We do not sell personal information.

OSINT and Platform Data

StratOps displays information derived from public or open-source signals and third-party sources. Public event, aircraft, maritime, infrastructure, or geospatial data shown in StratOps is separate from user account and billing data.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect user information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, and users should protect their account credentials.

Your Choices

Users may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of account-related information, subject to legal, billing, fraud-prevention, and security retention requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact bravo@battlespacex.com.
